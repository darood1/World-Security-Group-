Trainer development

Build your career as a security trainer.

Our SIA Trainer programme enquiries are designed for those considering a move into teaching security-related subjects. The precise qualification pathway depends on the subjects you intend to deliver, your existing qualifications and applicable awarding organisation and SIA requirements.

Ask about teaching qualifications, conflict management, physical intervention trainer development and refresher opportunities. We can discuss the applicable prerequisites and available delivery options.
