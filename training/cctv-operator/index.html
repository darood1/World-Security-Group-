Level 2 CCTV Operator

SIA licence-linked training for public space surveillance CCTV operators.

Course overview

What you'll learn

SIA licence-linked training for public space surveillance CCTV operators.

  • CCTV roles, responsibilities and operating procedures
  • Data protection, privacy and lawful surveillance
  • Control room communication and incident reporting
  • Camera operation, observation and tracking
  • Evidence handling and audit trails
  • Emergency response and professional conduct

Who is this course for?

People seeking to work in CCTV monitoring and public space surveillance roles.

Training and assessment

Knowledge assessments and practical CCTV operation assessments in line with the relevant qualification.

Course dates, delivery format, prerequisites, required documents and assessment arrangements will be confirmed by our team before you attend. Please check that this course meets your intended job role and qualification requirements.

Questions answered

Frequently asked questions

Do I need an SIA licence?

Many public space surveillance roles require an SIA CCTV licence; the requirement depends on the duties performed.

Is the course fully online?

Practical operation and assessment requirements normally require an appropriate supervised environment.

Will I use CCTV equipment?

The programme includes practical CCTV-related skills and assessment.

Can I work as a Door Supervisor with a CCTV licence?

No. Different licensable activities may require different licence categories.

What identification is required?

We provide an accepted-ID checklist before the course.
