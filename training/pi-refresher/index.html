Physical Intervention Refresher for Trainers

Refresher training to help existing physical intervention trainers maintain current knowledge, practical skills and safe delivery standards.

Course overview

What you'll learn

Refresher training to help existing physical intervention trainers maintain current knowledge, practical skills and safe delivery standards.

  • Updates to relevant guidance and professional practice
  • Risk assessment and safer intervention
  • Review and correction of practical techniques
  • Health, safety and welfare considerations
  • Coaching, observation and feedback
  • Recording competence and maintaining trainer currency

Who is this course for?

Existing qualified physical intervention trainers requiring an appropriate refresher or currency update.

Training and assessment

Observed practical performance and any required knowledge or trainer-delivery checks.

Course dates, delivery format, prerequisites, required documents and assessment arrangements will be confirmed by our team before you attend. Please check that this course meets your intended job role and qualification requirements.

Questions answered

Frequently asked questions

Who can attend?

This refresher is intended for trainers who already hold the relevant qualifications.

Do I need to bring certificates?

Yes. We may need to verify your original trainer qualification and previous refresher history.

Is this a new trainer qualification?

No. It is refresher training and does not replace initial trainer qualification requirements.

Will practical participation be required?

Yes, practical competency checks are normally part of physical intervention refresher training.

Will it meet my awarding organisation’s requirements?

Please tell us which awarding organisation or scheme you work with so we can confirm suitability before payment.
