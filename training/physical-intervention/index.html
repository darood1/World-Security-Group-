Level 3 Physical Intervention Trainer

Specialist development for eligible trainers intending to deliver physical intervention instruction.

Course overview

What you'll learn

Specialist development for eligible trainers intending to deliver physical intervention instruction.

  • Legal and ethical framework for physical intervention
  • Risk assessment and the dangers of restraint
  • Reducing the need for physical intervention
  • Safe practical skills and observation
  • Managing health, welfare and medical risks
  • Planning, demonstrating and assessing practical instruction

Who is this course for?

Experienced security professionals and trainers meeting applicable physical intervention trainer prerequisites.

Training and assessment

Theory, observed practical competence and trainer delivery assessment as required by the qualification.

Course dates, delivery format, prerequisites, required documents and assessment arrangements will be confirmed by our team before you attend. Please check that this course meets your intended job role and qualification requirements.

Questions answered

Frequently asked questions

Is this suitable for beginners?

This is a specialist trainer pathway. Previous sector and teaching qualifications may be required.

Will I practise physical skills?

Yes. Practical demonstration and safe participation are integral to this type of training.

Are there fitness or medical considerations?

Physical participation may be required. Disclose relevant participation concerns privately before booking so appropriate arrangements can be discussed.

Does completion allow me to teach immediately?

You must also satisfy the current awarding organisation, centre and SIA trainer requirements.

How often must I refresh?

Trainer currency and refresher requirements vary. We will confirm the applicable requirements for your pathway.
