Level 4 Internal Quality Assurance (IQA)

Develop the skills to monitor assessment quality, support assessors and maintain consistent standards across vocational qualifications.

Course overview

What you'll learn

Develop the skills to monitor assessment quality, support assessors and maintain consistent standards across vocational qualifications.

  • Principles and purposes of internal quality assurance
  • Risk-based sampling and planning
  • Reviewing assessment decisions and evidence
  • Standardisation and assessor support
  • Feedback, action planning and record keeping
  • Quality improvement and regulatory compliance

Who is this course for?

Assessors, trainers and quality staff seeking to develop internal quality assurance competence.

Training and assessment

Portfolio-based assessment; some qualification routes require access to live assessors and assessment decisions.

Course dates, delivery format, prerequisites, required documents and assessment arrangements will be confirmed by our team before you attend. Please check that this course meets your intended job role and qualification requirements.

Questions answered

Frequently asked questions

Is the course online?

Theory and portfolio support may be delivered remotely, subject to the qualification requirements.

Do I need access to assessors?

For qualifications assessing practical IQA competence, access to real assessment practice and assessors is normally necessary.

Do I need an assessor qualification?

Entry requirements vary by the specific IQA qualification and awarding organisation.

How will I be assessed?

Evidence may include sampling plans, feedback, records, reflective accounts and professional discussion.

Can I work as an IQA immediately?

Employment and sector-specific competence requirements are determined by the organisation and qualification being quality assured.
