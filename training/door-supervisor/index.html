Door Supervisor

SIA licence-linked training for people seeking work at licensed premises, events and other security environments.

Course overview

What you'll learn

SIA licence-linked training for people seeking work at licensed premises, events and other security environments.

  • The private security industry and the role of a door supervisor
  • Relevant legislation, licensing and professional conduct
  • Health and safety, fire safety and emergency response
  • Searching, access control, queue management and safeguarding
  • Conflict management, communication and de-escalation
  • Physical intervention: legal considerations and practical techniques

Who is this course for?

People aged 18 or over who want to apply for an SIA Door Supervisor licence.

Training and assessment

Classroom teaching, required self-study, knowledge examinations and practical physical intervention assessments.

Course dates, delivery format, prerequisites, required documents and assessment arrangements will be confirmed by our team before you attend. Please check that this course meets your intended job role and qualification requirements.

Questions answered

Frequently asked questions

Do I need to be 18?

You must be at least 18 to apply for an SIA licence. Check the training provider’s minimum enrolment age before booking.

Can I do the whole course online?

No. The qualification includes practical training and assessment that must be completed in person.

Will I receive an SIA licence automatically?

No. Successful qualification completion supports an SIA licence application; the SIA makes the licensing decision.

Is first aid required?

The SIA requires an accepted first aid qualification before starting licence-linked Door Supervisor training. Ask us to confirm the current accepted standard and validity.

How long is the course?

The programme includes classroom contact time and required self-study. We confirm the timetable and assessment dates before enrolment.
