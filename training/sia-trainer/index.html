SIA Trainer Course

A structured route for security professionals developing the qualifications and competence needed to teach SIA licence-linked subjects.

Course overview

What you'll learn

A structured route for security professionals developing the qualifications and competence needed to teach SIA licence-linked subjects.

  • Teaching practice and inclusive lesson delivery
  • Assessment and learner feedback
  • Conflict management trainer development
  • Physical intervention trainer pathways where relevant
  • Qualification and evidence requirements
  • Quality assurance, standardisation and continuing professional development

Who is this course for?

Experienced security professionals looking to move into training or broaden their approved teaching scope.

Training and assessment

Assessment depends on the components you need; this is a pathway rather than a single universal SIA qualification.

Course dates, delivery format, prerequisites, required documents and assessment arrangements will be confirmed by our team before you attend. Please check that this course meets your intended job role and qualification requirements.

Questions answered

Frequently asked questions

Is there one qualification called an SIA Trainer licence?

No. Trainer requirements depend on the licence-linked subject, teaching role and awarding organisation.

Do I need a teaching qualification?

A recognised teaching or training qualification is commonly part of the requirements; we will review your existing certificates.

Can I teach Door Supervision after this?

Only if you meet all relevant subject-specific trainer and awarding organisation requirements.

Are conflict management and physical intervention included?

The package contents vary. Confirm exactly which qualifications and assessments your booking includes.

Can I enrol with existing trainer qualifications?

Yes. Contact us for a gap analysis to avoid repeating qualifications you already hold.
