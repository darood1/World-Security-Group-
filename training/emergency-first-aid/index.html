Emergency First Aid at Work Course

Practical emergency first aid training to help you respond confidently to workplace incidents.

Course overview

What you'll learn

Practical emergency first aid training to help you respond confidently to workplace incidents.

  • Assessing an incident and keeping yourself and others safe
  • Checking responsiveness and calling emergency services
  • Adult CPR and use of an automated external defibrillator (AED)
  • Recovery position and management of an unconscious casualty
  • Recognising and responding to choking
  • Managing bleeding, shock and minor injuries

Who is this course for?

Employees, security personnel, supervisors and anyone designated to provide emergency first aid at work.

Training and assessment

Tutor-led practical training and assessment of key emergency first aid skills. Confirm course dates and the qualification specification before attendance.

Course dates, delivery format, prerequisites, required documents and assessment arrangements will be confirmed by our team before you attend. Please check that this course meets your intended job role and qualification requirements.

Questions answered

Frequently asked questions

Is this suitable for SIA Door Supervisor training?

An accepted first aid qualification may satisfy the SIA first aid prerequisite, provided it meets current SIA rules and is valid when required. Confirm before booking.

Is this the same as the three-day First Aid at Work course?

No. Emergency First Aid at Work generally covers a narrower range of emergency skills.

Will I practise CPR?

Yes, practical CPR and AED skills form part of the training.

Can I complete the course entirely online?

No. Practical first aid skills must be assessed in person.

How long is the certificate valid?

Workplace first aid certificates are typically valid for three years; check the exact qualification and refresher guidance.
