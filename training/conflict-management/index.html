Level 3 Award in the Delivery of Conflict Management Training

Specialist trainer development for those who want to teach conflict management safely and effectively.

Course overview

What you'll learn

Specialist trainer development for those who want to teach conflict management safely and effectively.

  • Causes and stages of conflict
  • Dynamic risk assessment and early intervention
  • Verbal and non-verbal communication
  • De-escalation, personal safety and professional boundaries
  • Planning conflict management training sessions
  • Facilitating scenarios and evaluating learner performance

Who is this course for?

Existing or prospective security trainers who meet the relevant teaching and sector entry requirements.

Training and assessment

Knowledge and practical trainer-delivery evidence, including planned teaching and scenario-based assessment as required.

Course dates, delivery format, prerequisites, required documents and assessment arrangements will be confirmed by our team before you attend. Please check that this course meets your intended job role and qualification requirements.

Questions answered

Frequently asked questions

Is this the same as a Door Supervisor course?

No. This course develops skills for delivering conflict management training rather than qualifying as a Door Supervisor.

Do I need a teaching qualification?

Trainer eligibility depends on the applicable awarding organisation and SIA requirements. We will check your qualifications before enrolment.

Will there be practical scenarios?

Scenario-based learning and trainer delivery are important parts of the programme.

Can it be completed entirely online?

Ask us about the current delivery model; practical trainer assessment may require live participation.

Does it guarantee trainer approval?

No. Approval depends on the relevant awarding organisation and centre requirements.
