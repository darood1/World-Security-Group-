Level 3 Award in Education and Training (AET)

An introductory teaching qualification for aspiring tutors, instructors and vocational trainers.

Course overview

What you'll learn

An introductory teaching qualification for aspiring tutors, instructors and vocational trainers.

  • Roles, responsibilities and boundaries in education and training
  • Inclusive teaching and meeting diverse learner needs
  • Planning structured sessions and learning objectives
  • Teaching and learning approaches
  • Assessment methods and constructive feedback
  • Reflective practice and professional development

Who is this course for?

New or aspiring trainers, subject experts and people moving into adult or vocational education.

Training and assessment

Written assignments, planning evidence and a micro-teaching activity; specific arrangements are confirmed before enrolment.

Course dates, delivery format, prerequisites, required documents and assessment arrangements will be confirmed by our team before you attend. Please check that this course meets your intended job role and qualification requirements.

Questions answered

Frequently asked questions

Do I need previous teaching experience?

Previous teaching experience is not normally required for this introductory award.

Can I study online?

Learning activities may be offered remotely, but the required micro-teaching and assessment arrangements must be completed as directed.

Does this qualify me to teach every subject?

No. Sector-specific teaching, assessor and technical qualifications may also be required.

How is it assessed?

Typically through a portfolio of evidence and micro-teaching.

How long does it take?

The completion schedule depends on delivery mode and assessment progress. Ask for the current timetable.
