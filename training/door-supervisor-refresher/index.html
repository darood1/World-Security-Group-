Door Supervisor Refresher

Update the knowledge and practical skills required for eligible Door Supervisor licence renewals.

Course overview

What you'll learn

Update the knowledge and practical skills required for eligible Door Supervisor licence renewals.

  • Updated legal responsibilities and industry practice
  • Searching and screening procedures
  • Responding to vulnerable people and safeguarding concerns
  • Terror threat awareness and emergency response
  • Conflict management and communication
  • Physical intervention skills, safety and risk reduction

Who is this course for?

Existing Door Supervisor licence holders approaching renewal, subject to current SIA eligibility rules.

Training and assessment

Tutor-led refresher activities, multiple-choice knowledge assessment and practical skills assessment.

Course dates, delivery format, prerequisites, required documents and assessment arrangements will be confirmed by our team before you attend. Please check that this course meets your intended job role and qualification requirements.

Questions answered

Frequently asked questions

Who needs refresher training?

Door Supervisor licence holders renewing their licence must check the SIA’s current refresher training requirements.

Do I need first aid?

The SIA requires an accepted first aid qualification before Door Supervisor refresher training. Confirm your evidence with us before booking.

Is it entirely online?

No. Practical components require attendance.

Will my licence renew automatically?

No. You must submit the relevant renewal application and satisfy the SIA’s checks.

What should I bring?

Bring accepted identification, any required first aid evidence and other documents specified in your joining instructions.
