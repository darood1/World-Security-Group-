Our story

Established in 2017

World Security Group was established in 2017 to provide professional security services for businesses and organisations. Our approach is built around understanding the environment, assessing operational needs and delivering appropriate protective support.

We support enquiries for retail guarding, static security, gatehouse operations, construction security, mobile patrols, vacant property protection, fire watch, concierge security and additional specialist services.

Our training provision complements our operational focus by supporting learners interested in security careers and trainer development.
