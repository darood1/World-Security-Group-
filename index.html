Who we are

Focused on protecting what matters.

World Security Group was established in 2017 with a commitment to dependable, professional security provision. We work with organisations seeking a practical security partner who understands the importance of safe premises, responsive communication and well-presented personnel.

From retail environments and construction sites to offices, logistics facilities and vacant properties, we take a site-specific approach to understanding risks and recommending proportionate coverage.
