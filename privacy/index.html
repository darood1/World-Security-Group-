Privacy Notice

How information submitted through our website is handled.

Website privacy notice

World Security Group receives the personal information you submit through our enquiry, quotation and careers forms, such as your name, contact details, address, SIA licence number (for applicants), and CV where supplied.

Why we use your information

We use submitted information to respond to enquiries, prepare quotations, discuss training, assess applications and manage related correspondence. Our usual lawful basis is taking steps at your request before a contract, or our legitimate interests in responding to business enquiries and recruiting staff, as appropriate.

Form delivery

Website forms use a third-party form delivery service (FormSubmit) to forward submissions to our email inbox. This may involve processing outside the UK. Before launching, World Security Group should verify the provider's privacy terms, international transfer safeguards and suitability for handling CVs and licence numbers.

Retention and rights

We should keep information only for as long as necessary for the relevant enquiry or recruitment purpose, in line with our applicable retention policy. You can contact us to request access, correction, deletion or other rights where applicable.

Contact

Email info@worldsecuritygroup.co.uk for privacy enquiries. You may also raise concerns with the UK Information Commissioner's Office.

This is a starter privacy notice and must be reviewed and completed by the business before publication, including legal entity details, retention periods and processor arrangements.
